The preview asked whether the Mercedes was genuinely the fastest car or simply
the best driven one. Barcelona answered by handing the race to a Ferrari, and
gave <DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> the win he had been chasing since he put on
red overalls.

It is his first victory for Ferrari, his first Grand Prix win since Belgium in
2024, and the 106th of his career. At 41 he becomes the oldest driver to win a
Formula 1 race since Jack Brabham in 1970 and the first over the age of 40
since Nigel Mansell in 1994. The podium behind him -
<DriverChip ref="russell" /> second, <DriverChip ref="norris" /> third - was the
first all-British rostrum since the 1968 United States Grand Prix, and the
first podium of any single nationality since San Marino in 1983.

## The race

<DriverChip ref="russell" /> had [pole](/races/2026/7/) at 1:14.679, Mercedes'
seventh in a row, and led the opening stint. The race was decided in the
Ferrari pit wall's spreadsheet rather than on the road.

On lap 28 Ferrari brought <DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> in for a second stop
and fitted mediums, committing him to a three-stop in a race the Mercedes
drivers were running in two. The pace advantage was immediate. It still needed
the timing to fall right, and on lap 41 it did: <DriverChip ref="alonso" />
stopped on track with a battery failure, the virtual safety car came out, and
Ferrari called Hamilton in for hard tyres at exactly the moment a pit stop cost
almost nothing. He rejoined ahead of Russell and stayed there, winning by
19.561s. It is the largest winning margin of the 2026 season so far.

Track temperatures were above 50 °C before the formation lap and degradation
was severe all afternoon, which is how a race with two virtual safety cars and
no full safety car still ended with everyone from sixth place back at least a
lap down.

## Antonelli's first blank Sunday

<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> was running second with five laps to go when he
attacked <DriverChip ref="russell" /> for the place, went wheel to wheel with
his team-mate, and came out ahead. He also came out with a damaged front wing.
He slowed shortly afterwards and stopped with a mechanical problem on lap 61,
classified sixteenth. He had already picked up a five-second penalty for
exceeding track limits. Six rounds of scoring at every one, and the run ended
with a move on his own team-mate that neither Mercedes needed.

Almost simultaneously, on lap 62, <DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> stopped with a
power steering failure, triggering the second virtual safety car. His weekend
had gone wrong a day earlier: he lost the Ferrari at Turn 4 in Q3, ran through
the gravel and hit the barriers, set no time in the session and started tenth.

The oddest retirement of the day belonged to <DriverChip ref="hulkenberg" />,
who was running strongly from ninth on the grid when a stray rock, kicked up by
<DriverChip ref="lawson" />'s Racing Bulls, struck the safety switch on the Audi
and shut the electronics down on lap 29.

The leader's total did not move. Everyone else's did.
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> stays on 156 and watches a 66-point cushion
become 41 in a single afternoon, the first time all season the gap has closed.
<DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> is second on 115 and has now outscored his former
team-mate in consecutive rounds, with <DriverChip ref="russell" /> third on 106.

Ferrari's 25 points against Mercedes' 18 barely dents a constructors' lead that
still reads 72. But the number that matters is the one at the top of the
drivers' table, and for the first time in 2026 it is going in the wrong
direction. [Austria](/races/2026/8/) is next.
