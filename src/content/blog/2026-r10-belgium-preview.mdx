[Silverstone](/races/2026/9/) ended with the championship leader fifteenth in a
damaged car and his advantage cut almost in half. Two weeks later the season
arrives at [Spa-Francorchamps](/circuits/spa/), which is the last place a team
whose Sundays have started going wrong wants to be. Seven kilometres of
Ardennes forest, 44 laps, and a lap so long that a weather front can sit over
one end of the circuit while the other stays dry.

<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> still leads, and by a race win's worth of points.
But the trend has turned: he has been beaten in each of the last two rounds, and
for the first time in 2026 the cars behind him arrive with the momentum.

Twenty-five points is the smallest the lead has been since the start of May.
It was 66 after Monaco. Barcelona took 25 off it in an afternoon when
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> retired, Austria trimmed one more, and Silverstone
took another fifteen. <DriverChip ref="russell" /> and <DriverChip ref="hamilton" />
are now separated by seven points in the fight for second, which is its own race
inside the race, and <DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> has moved clear of the McLarens
after winning at Silverstone.

## What Spa asks of the cars

**Aerodynamic efficiency, not maximum downforce:** the run from La Source
through Eau Rouge and up the Kemmel straight is nearly two kilometres of full
throttle, then the middle sector demands genuine load through Pouhon and the
Speaker's Corner sweeps. Every team runs a wing level they are unhappy with in
one sector or the other. **Power unit:** more of this lap is spent at full
throttle than almost anywhere, so the 2026 units - and how well each team
manages the electrical deployment across a 7 km lap - matter more here than at
any circuit since Monza will. **Tyre energy:** degradation is high, the corners
are long and fast, and a two-stop is normal.

<PullQuote>“You come out of Eau Rouge with the car light and the throttle pinned and you have another twenty seconds of straight to think about whether that was wise.”</PullQuote>

Then there is the weather, which at Spa is less a variable than a participant.
Rain over Malmedy while the pit straight stays dry is a normal Sunday here, and
the circuit has produced more strategy-inverting afternoons than anywhere on
the calendar. For a championship leader carrying a 25-point cushion, that is a
threat. For the two men behind him, it is the best tool they have.
