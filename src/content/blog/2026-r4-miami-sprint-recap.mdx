Four rounds of 2026, and until Saturday lunchtime every pole, every win and
every sprint had belonged to Mercedes. Then <DriverChip ref="norris" /> put
his McLaren on sprint pole, converted the run to Turn 1, and led all
nineteen laps. <DriverChip ref="piastri" /> made it a 1-2. The reigning
champion's first victory of any kind in 2026, McLaren's first of the season -
and the first crack in the Mercedes wall.

The win itself was straightforward - Norris cleared off at the start and was
never headed, with Piastri riding shotgun and keeping
<DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> at arm's length for the 1-2. The upgrade package
McLaren pulled forward from Imola - seven items, the biggest any team has
brought this year - looked worth every hour of the rush job. On a hot,
low-grip afternoon, nobody else had an answer.

The mess was all behind them. <DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> launched poorly
from second, fell behind Piastri and Leclerc, and then drove like a man
having exactly the Saturday he was having: four track-limits violations, a
black-and-white flag, and finally a five-second penalty that dropped him
from fourth on the road to sixth. Along the way
<DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> and <DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> staged
the sprint's best fight - Verstappen completed a pass off the track at
Turn 11, was told to hand it back, did, and then re-passed cleanly a lap
later to take fifth.

## Audi's day from hell

Neither Audi saw the finish - or, in one case, the start.
<DriverChip ref="hulkenberg" />'s engine let go in smoke and flame on the way
to the grid, his slot left empty. <DriverChip ref="bortoleto" /> finished
eleventh on the road and was then disqualified: the engine's intake air
pressure had crept past the 4.8-bar maximum during one hot lap, and a
technical breach is a technical breach, however small. Add
<DriverChip ref="arvid_lindblad" />'s non-start - relegated to the pit lane
because his RB was left uncovered overnight, then withdrawn with a technical
issue before he could even take that up - and a third of the midfield never
raced at all.

Before the start the grid stood for a minute's silence for Alex Zanardi,
whose death had been announced the previous day.

Eight points for Norris, none of the big ones for the leader:
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" />'s cushion over <DriverChip ref="russell" /> is
down to seven points, the closest the top of the table has been since
Melbourne. <DriverChip ref="bearman" />'s 17 still have him ahead of a
Verstappen whose car remains the story Red Bull don't want to talk about.

The [Miami Grand Prix](/races/2026/4/) runs on Sunday, and the question it
has to answer is simple: was this McLaren's weekend, or McLaren's turning
point?
