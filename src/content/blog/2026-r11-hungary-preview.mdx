Two weeks ago the title fight was down to 25 points and the chasers had the
momentum. Then [Spa](/races/2026/10/) happened: pole, win, and a championship
lead back out to 45. Whatever Silverstone suggested about
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> being catchable, the Ardennes un-suggested it.

Now the season stops for a month, but not before the [Hungaroring](/circuits/hungaroring/) -
seventy laps of a circuit with fourteen corners, one DRS zone and a reputation
earned honestly. Budapest is where a fast car that qualifies badly stays
stuck, where the strategy variance is among the highest of the year, and where
the last five runnings have produced four different winners. It is the least
predictable race on a calendar that has, so far, been extremely predictable.

Spa restored the order at the top and scrambled it just below.
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> won from pole for his sixth victory in ten
rounds. <DriverChip ref="russell" /> did not complete a lap, which handed second
in the championship back to <DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> by five points.
<DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> finished second and is now 126, closer to the top
three than to the McLarens. And the fight for fifth has compressed to nothing:
<DriverChip ref="norris" /> on 103, <DriverChip ref="piastri" /> on 92,
<DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> on 91.

## What the Hungaroring asks of the cars

**Maximum downforce, minimum straight:** the longest straight is 700 m and
there is one DRS zone. Teams bolt on the biggest wing they own and accept that
the pit straight is a place to recover, not to attack. **Front-end rotation:**
the lap is a chain of medium and slow corners that flow into each other, so a
car that will not turn in the middle of a corner never gets a clean exit
anywhere. **Heat:** Budapest in late July is reliably the hottest race of the
European season, which loads the tyres, the brakes and the cooling all at once -
and the 2026 power units have been at their least happy on hot afternoons.

<PullQuote>“There is no straight to fix your mistakes on. You just carry the lap you have until it ends.”</PullQuote>

Track position is the whole game. Overtaking here is rated among the lowest on
the calendar, and while the 2026 cars follow better than their predecessors,
nobody expects that to turn the Hungaroring into a passing circuit. The
undercut is powerful, the safety car is common, and the team that gets its
Saturday right usually gets to dictate Sunday.
