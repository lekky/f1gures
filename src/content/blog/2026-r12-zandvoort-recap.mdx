The last Dutch Grand Prix got the full Zandvoort send-off: rain an hour
before the start, a home hero in the wall before the first lap was done,
and a winner who settled it with one of the passes of the season - around
the outside of two cars at once into Tarzan. <DriverChip ref="norris" />
won from pole for the second race running, and this one took two hours and
four minutes to earn.

## One lap of orange

Heavy rain stopped just before the pit lane opened, leaving a damp track,
slicks all round and a formation lap behind the safety car. At the end of
lap 1 <DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> put a wheel on the damp inside
edge of the banked final corner, lost the rear on throttle, and hit the
outside barrier hard enough to tear a wheel off. He walked away; the race
did not. Red flag, and grandstands packed solid with orange watching their
man's last home Grand Prix end before it began.
<DriverChip ref="bortoleto" /> spun a full 360 avoiding the wreck and
somehow kept going. The restart then delayed itself:
<DriverChip ref="bearman" />'s Haas stopped with a power-unit failure on
the way to the grid, forcing an extra formation lap before the standing
restart on lap 5.

## The race Antonelli controlled and lost

<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> won the restart, beating Norris into Tarzan,
and for fifty laps managed the gap the way he has managed the whole season -
never letting the McLaren close enough to use its overtake deployment.
Strategy undid him. Norris ran seven laps longer in the middle stint, took
fresh hards on lap 48, and arrived on lap 54 with a tyre advantage just as
Antonelli, rears fading, lunged at the off-strategy, not-yet-stopped
<DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> into Turn 1. Norris braked later than both
and swept around the outside of the pair of them - Mercedes and Ferrari
dispatched in one corner. When Hamilton ran wide at Turn 10 later that lap,
the lead was Norris's, and an 11.5s win followed.

Mercedes threw what was left at it. Antonelli took softs under the virtual
safety car called for <DriverChip ref="ocon" />'s stricken Haas on lap 56,
and on lap 65 the pit wall ordered <DriverChip ref="russell" /> to hand
second place to his teammate - Russell questioned it, then obliged. The
chase lasted until the softs faded and a second VSC, for the Williams
teammates' collision at Turn 5 on lap 70, ended it. <DriverChip ref="sainz" />
collected a 10-second penalty for putting <DriverChip ref="albon" /> out of
the race; Ferrari's afternoon peaked with Hamilton asking to be waved past
Leclerc, being refused, and offering the radio's driest line - "Great way
of wasting time guys, good job" - before finishing ahead of him anyway.
<DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> took fifth and the fastest lap, 1:14.230.

The drive of the midfield was <DriverChip ref="alonso" />: eighteenth on
the grid after a Q1 exit, ninth at the flag on a one-stop, on a day his
teammate retired with early damage. <DriverChip ref="lawson" /> took
seventh on his Red Bull debut, surviving a 10-second penalty for failing
to slow under yellow flags, and <DriverChip ref="hulkenberg" /> dodged the
lap-1 chaos to finish eighth from thirteenth.

## The DNFs

- <DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> - lap 1, crashed at the banked final
  corner, red flag
- <DriverChip ref="bearman" /> - power-unit failure on the way to the
  lap-5 restart
- <DriverChip ref="stroll" /> - around lap 45, retired after early damage
  left the Aston Martin sliding everywhere
- <DriverChip ref="ocon" /> - lap 56, power unit, the second Haas engine of
  the day
- <DriverChip ref="bottas" /> - late on, hydraulics jamming the Cadillac's
  active rear wing
- <DriverChip ref="albon" /> - lap 70, collision with Sainz, classified 17th

Second place means <DriverChip ref="antonelli" />'s lead holds at 59 - now
over two drivers at once, with <DriverChip ref="russell" /> and
<DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> level on 183 and Russell ahead on wins.
<DriverChip ref="norris" /> is the season's form driver: two poles, two
wins, and 56 points from the last two-and-a-bit rounds have carried him
past Leclerc to fourth. Verstappen's zero leaves him 130 adrift, and even
the Orange Army filed out knowing what that number means.

Zandvoort leaves the calendar with a McLaren hat-trick of Dutch GP wins -
Norris 2024, Piastri 2025, Norris 2026. [Monza](/races/2026/13/) is next
week, and for the first time since March the question is not whether anyone
can beat Antonelli, but whether Norris already is.
