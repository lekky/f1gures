The preview said the Hungaroring was the least predictable race on a very
predictable calendar. It delivered: the first non-Mercedes pole of 2026, by
0.012s. The first McLaren Grand Prix win of the season, decided in the pit
cycle. A second place for a Red Bull its driver had called completely broken
the day before. And a gearbox that chose lap 56 of 70 to end the other
McLaren's afternoon.

For <DriverChip ref="norris" /> it ends two droughts at once. No one but a
Mercedes driver had taken pole in ten rounds of 2026; his 1:17.207 stopped
that streak. And the reigning champion had not won a Grand Prix since
[São Paulo last November](/races/2025/21/) - eight months, half a title
defence, and a season of watching a 19-year-old drive away with his crown.
Career win twelve, a second consecutive Hungarian GP win, and McLaren's
fourteenth in Hungary, extending their own record.

## Saturday set the trap

Qualifying finished Norris, <DriverChip ref="hamilton" />,
<DriverChip ref="leclerc" />, <DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> - and then the
stewards got involved. Hamilton dropped three places for crawling on the
racing line and ruining <DriverChip ref="piastri" />'s final Q3 lap;
Antonelli dropped three for failing to lift for the yellow flags thrown when
<DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> spun his Red Bull at the final corner.
So the grid read Norris, Leclerc, Piastri, Verstappen - who had qualified
sixth and radioed that the car was "completely broken aerodynamically" -
with Hamilton fifth, <DriverChip ref="russell" /> sixth, Antonelli seventh.

## The race that turned at the pit stops

Piastri won the start, cutting back at Turn 2 to lead lap 1, and controlled
the first half on a track sitting around 55°C. Norris asked for the
undercut; McLaren said no. What he got instead was the overcut, gift-wrapped
by <DriverChip ref="sainz" />: when Piastri stopped on lap 33 he came out
behind the Williams, which ignored blue flags long enough for the two to
touch at Turn 2 and for Piastri to offer the radio's line of the day -
"Get out of the way, you idiot!" Norris stayed out, pitted with clear air,
and came back ahead. The stewards gave Sainz five seconds; Piastri got five
too, for the contact, and never got the chance to pay them - the gearbox
failed on lap 56 while he was running second, and the McLaren rolled to a
stop.

The resulting virtual safety car handed Verstappen his podium. He had
climbed to the podium fight through the pit cycles, stayed out under the
VSC for track position, then took fresh softs late and brought a car he'd
spent the weekend swearing at home second, 15.080s behind. Antonelli did
what champions-elect do: from seventh, via an abandoned one-stop and a
lap-23 bail-out, he collected third when the race came back to him.

Behind the podium, Hamilton crossed the line fourth and was classified
fifth: five seconds added for pit-lane speeding, measured at 0.1 km/h over
the 80 km/h limit. It promoted Leclerc, who also took the fastest lap.
<DriverChip ref="hadjar" /> was best of the rest in sixth, and Russell
finished seventh after his anti-stall engaged at the start and dropped him
to the back of the field - his recovery was worth a minute of traffic on a
circuit that hates recoveries. Only seven cars finished on the lead lap.

## The DNFs

- <DriverChip ref="bottas" /> - early on, left-front brake caught fire, the
  Cadillac crawling back to the pits in flames - at the race Cadillac had
  targeted with brake-cooling upgrades
- <DriverChip ref="perez" /> - lap 48, front-left suspension, his second
  consecutive suspension DNF after Spa. He had started from the pit lane
  after overnight setup changes
- <DriverChip ref="piastri" /> - lap 56, gearbox, running second

Third place means <DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> goes into the summer break
50 points clear of <DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> and 59 clear of
<DriverChip ref="russell" /> - the biggest August cushion any leader has
carried in years, exactly as the preview suggested pole-position form would
produce. The fifth-place knot the preview flagged has been untied in one
afternoon: <DriverChip ref="norris" /> took 25 points from Piastri's zero
and Verstappen's 18 and now sits fifth on 128, a win from fourth.

F1 sleeps for a month. It resumes at [Zandvoort](/races/2026/12/) in late
August - a sprint weekend, a home crowd for Verstappen, and the last Dutch
Grand Prix on the calendar. If the second half of 2026 is going to be a
contest, someone has to start it there.
