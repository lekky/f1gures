The first sprint ever run at Zandvoort will also be the last, and it
belonged to <DriverChip ref="russell" /> from the first metre. Sprint pole,
a clean launch, and twenty-four laps of control around the dunes while a
North Sea squall stalked the circuit and arrived, obligingly, just as he
took the flag. His third sprint win of 2026 - no one else has more than one -
and his own verdict was honest: one more lap of rain and it might have been
a very different story.

On a circuit where the preview called overtaking a qualifying problem, the
sprint mostly agreed. The exception was <DriverChip ref="leclerc" />, who
made the move of the race on lap 18 - by the soft-tyred Ferrari on
<DriverChip ref="norris" />'s McLaren into Turn 1 - and pulled away to
finish 1.360s behind Russell. <DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> spent the final
lap filling Norris's mirrors into Tarzan but couldn't make the podium spot
stick; he'd been carrying floor damage since a trip through the SQ1 gravel
on Friday, which made fourth a decent salvage. <DriverChip ref="piastri" />
held off <DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> for fifth, the home crowd
roaring a sixth place like a podium.

The stand-in seats told quieter stories. <DriverChip ref="lawson" />
finished eleventh on his first Red Bull start, exactly where he began;
<DriverChip ref="tsunoda" />, back in an RB race seat, was
thirteenth. <DriverChip ref="hulkenberg" />'s Audi developed a power-unit
problem early and toured home laps down after rejoining for data.
<DriverChip ref="perez" />'s Cadillac never made the grid at all - a
technical failure before the formation lap saw the car wheeled back, and he
joined from the pit lane three laps late to run in formation at the rear.
<DriverChip ref="sainz" /> and <DriverChip ref="alonso" /> also started
from the pit lane after parc fermé setup changes.

Five points for Antonelli keep the lead at a heavy 53 over
<DriverChip ref="hamilton" />, with Russell's eight closing him to within
three of the Ferrari. The math of the sprint is marginal; the message is
not. Mercedes have now won four of the season's five sprints, and Saturdays
keep being the days the chasers can't land a blow.

Qualifying for the [Dutch Grand Prix](/races/2026/12/) follows this
afternoon, with rain in the air and the last Zandvoort Sunday of them all
tomorrow.
