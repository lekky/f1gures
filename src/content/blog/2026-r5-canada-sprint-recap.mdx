The first sprint ever held at Circuit Gilles Villeneuve delivered the thing
Mercedes have avoided all season: their two drivers using the same piece of
track at the same time. <DriverChip ref="russell" /> won it from sprint pole,
his second sprint win of 2026 - but the story was the lap-six moment when
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> hung his car around the outside of his
teammate into the first chicane, found the door firmly shut, banged wheels
and bounced across the grass.

The contact cost Antonelli second to <DriverChip ref="norris" />, and his
follow-up lunge at the Turn 8 chicane ended in a lock-up and another trip
across the grass. The radio traffic wrote the headlines for him: Russell was
"very naughty", a penalty was demanded, and the reply from the pit wall was,
in effect, stop moaning. The stewards agreed with the pit wall - no
investigation stuck - and Antonelli went to see Toto Wolff about it as soon
as he climbed out. Russell's view: nobody gets overtaken around the outside
of Turn 1, so the defence was safe. Norris spent the closing laps within DRS
range and threw one last look into Turn 1 on the final lap that never quite
stuck. At the flag, 1.272s and 1.843s covered the three of them.

Behind, <DriverChip ref="piastri" /> and <DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> were
split by a tenth, and <DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> brushed the Wall of
Champions defending fourth late on - he dropped to sixth in the final laps
with the wall's paint on his sidepod. <DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" />
took seventh from seventh: quietly, at a power circuit, the Red Bull looked
less lost than it has all year.

## The midfield that mostly started in the pit lane

Four cars - <DriverChip ref="albon" />, <DriverChip ref="gasly" />,
<DriverChip ref="bearman" /> and <DriverChip ref="bottas" /> - started from
the pit lane for parc fermé breaches, and all finished a lap down in a
23-lap race with no safety car to close the field. <DriverChip ref="stroll" />
joined them at the back and finished sixteenth at his home race.
<DriverChip ref="hadjar" />'s afternoon died early: an engine problem from
eighth on the grid, two pit visits, and three laps lost circulating for
data. His teammate <DriverChip ref="arvid_lindblad" /> collected the final
point in eighth - the first sprint the RB rookie has actually finished,
having retired in Shanghai and never started in Miami, and he did it on the
hard tyre nobody else chose. <DriverChip ref="alonso" /> was the only
retirement, ending a weekend that had already included a crash at the end
of SQ1. <DriverChip ref="hulkenberg" /> earned a 10-second penalty for
leaving the track and gaining an advantage.

Eight more points for <DriverChip ref="russell" /> cut the gap to 18 - and
for the first time this season, the intra-Mercedes fight looks like an
actual fight, elbows included. <DriverChip ref="norris" /> has quietly
outscored everyone but Antonelli since the Miami sprint - 25 points to the
leader's 31, with Russell on 20 over the same stretch.

The [Canadian Grand Prix](/races/2026/5/) runs on Sunday, with qualifying
still to come this evening - and a team debrief in Brackley that suddenly
matters more than either.
