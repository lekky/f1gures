The preview wondered whether Spa would punish a team whose Sundays had started
coming apart. It did, but not the team anyone expected. Mercedes lost a car on
the first lap and still left the Ardennes with a bigger championship lead than
they arrived with, because <DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> took
[pole](/races/2026/10/) by 0.317s, lost the lead to a virtual safety car, and
then went and took it back on the road.

## Lap one

<DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> arrived at the first part of Les Combes with his
left-front alongside <DriverChip ref="russell" />'s right-rear and could not
hold the gap. The contact spun the Mercedes into the gravel and ended Russell's
race before he had completed a lap. The stewards judged Hamilton wholly
responsible for failing to maintain sufficient separation and gave him a
five-second penalty. Russell later revealed the car had been carrying an
electrical fault that left his battery roughly 35 per cent below its expected
state of charge, so he had no deployment down the Kemmel Straight to defend
with in the first place.

The safety car came out on lap 2. Elsewhere on that opening lap
<DriverChip ref="ocon" /> made contact with both <DriverChip ref="sainz" /> and
<DriverChip ref="bearman" /> and picked up a front-left puncture.

## The race

It stayed dry. Rain was reported approaching during the formation lap and never
arrived, leaving a cool, overcast afternoon and a straight fight.

Two virtual safety cars around laps 19 and 20 reshaped it. Ferrari
double-stacked both cars during the second, and <DriverChip ref="leclerc" />
emerged in the lead with the cheapest pit stop of the afternoon.
<DriverChip ref="antonelli" /> spent the next dozen laps hunting him down,
arrived on his gearbox on lap 33, and used the Mercedes' straight-line speed
and overtake deployment to go past on the Kemmel Straight before Les Combes on
lap 34.

Leclerc did not go away. He sat within a second for several laps and was still
there at the flag, 1.952s back, the third-closest finish of the season. He
simply never got another look.
<DriverChip ref="max_verstappen" /> completed the podium from second on the
grid, 11.586s down. On lap 8 Leclerc and <DriverChip ref="piastri" /> touched at
Les Combes; the stewards took no action, ruling Piastri's front axle had not
drawn ahead.

## The two grid penalties, and what they were worth

Two of the best drives of the afternoon started from the back half of the grid,
and neither was the driver's fault.

<DriverChip ref="hadjar" /> qualified tenth and started twenty-first, carrying a
thirty-place penalty after Red Bull fitted a fifth internal combustion engine,
turbocharger and exhaust. He ran a three-stop, took the tactical route through
the early safety car, and finished sixth. That is a gain of fifteen places, the
largest grid-to-finish improvement anyone has managed in 2026.

<DriverChip ref="norris" /> qualified third and started thirteenth after a
ten-place penalty for a fourth control electronics unit. He recovered to
seventh despite a problem with the left-rear wheel at his second stop that
dropped him to eighth, and then took the fastest lap of the race on the final
lap with a 1:48.890.

<DriverChip ref="bortoleto" /> took eighth for Audi, rookie
<DriverChip ref="arvid_lindblad" /> ninth, and <DriverChip ref="colapinto" />
tenth. Ferrari were summoned after the race over an alleged unsafe release, the
right-front of Hamilton's car having made low-speed contact with a mechanic
during his stop.

Antonelli's pole was Mercedes' tenth from ten in 2026, and the 0.317s margin
was the largest of the season.

Twenty-five points for the winner and none for the man who was second in the
table takes the lead from 25 back out to 45.
<DriverChip ref="hamilton" /> is second again on 159, five ahead of
<DriverChip ref="russell" />, who has now been passed in the standings by the
driver who put him out of the race. <DriverChip ref="leclerc" /> is fourth on
126 and closer to the podium places than to the McLarens. Fifth is a three-way
knot: Norris 103, Piastri 92, Verstappen 91.

Six wins, six poles, and a lead restored in one afternoon.
[Hungary](/races/2026/11/) closes the first half next weekend before the summer
break, on a circuit where Saturday matters more than almost anywhere - which,
on this evidence, is not what the chasers wanted to hear.
